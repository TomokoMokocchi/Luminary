Connection help

Checking connection

A TURN relay forwards game traffic when your browser cannot reach the game server directly. This can add delay or make movement less smooth. The game automatically prefers a direct connection when one is available.

  1. If permitted, disconnect your VPN or proxy, then rejoin.
  2. Try another network, such as home Wi-Fi or a mobile hotspot. Work, school, and guest networks may restrict game traffic.
  3. Ask your network administrator whether outgoing WebRTC UDP traffic is allowed. Keep your firewall enabled; port forwarding is usually unnecessary.

After changing networks, use Leave room and join again. This ends your current play session. The game will try a direct connection and keep the relay available if needed; a button cannot bypass a network restriction.

A wired connection or stronger Wi-Fi may reduce lag even if a relay is still needed.

How to play

Move W/A/S/D or arrow keys · Space to jump · Click the arena to lock the mouse when enabled; otherwise drag to look

Fight Click or F to attack · Double-click to lunge · Use the hotbar keys to equip · Backspace to drop · Touch a loose tool to pick it up

Camera & chat Move the mouse to look · Q/E to turn left/right · Scroll to zoom · V for first person · Shift for Shift Lock · / or Enter to chat · Tab for players

Touch Drag the movement joystick; use Jump and Attack on the right. Drag the world to look around. Tap the chat icon to open chat, or a name in the player list to mute chat.

Gamepad Left stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps, RT or X attacks, LB/RB changes tools, Y equips, right-stick click changes view, left-stick click drops, Menu opens this menu. D-pad up/down zooms; in the menu use the D-pad and A. Double-tap attack to lunge.
